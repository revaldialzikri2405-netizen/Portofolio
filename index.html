A little about me

People matter.
Good systems do too.

I work as a Network Support Specialist at J&T Cargo. A lot of my work involves keeping information and day-to-day operations in order: I handle operational reports, partner and vendor contracts, attendance and salary reports, and data for area programs. I also maintain records for 53 Blind Van vehicles across West Sumatra, submit requests for new outlet openings, and have handled more than 50 partner contracts, from new agreements to renewals.

Before that, I was a Network Training Specialist. I handled SOP reports for 55 outlets and ran weekly online refresher sessions for their 55 admins. I also helped organize in-person training in Padang, calculated KPI results for Regional Managers and area PICs, and hosted a company event with more than 200 people from different organizations. That experience showed me how much clear communication and good coordination matter when people are learning and working together.

I’m drawn to Human Resources because I want to support people as they grow in their careers while helping organizations move forward. I see HR as the link between the two: bringing in the right people, helping them develop, and making the everyday processes around work clear and dependable. I’m open to growing in recruitment, learning and development, or generalist HR, and keen to keep learning as I find where I can contribute most.

What I want to build

People supported by clear processes.

I’d like to help create a workplace where people know what’s expected, can get the support they need, and have room to grow. To me, that often starts with simple things done well: clear communication, useful training, accurate information, and follow-through.

  • Training and communication people can use in their work
  • Recruitment and employee processes handled with care
  • Accurate people data employees and managers can rely on
  • Day-to-day coordination that makes work easier for people and teams

People & Performance Reporting
